# Safety Disclaimer

**Effective date: August 15, 2026**  
**Document version: 1.1.0**

WiggleGlow is a sensory entertainment application. It is not a medical device, therapeutic product, diagnostic tool, treatment, substitute for professional care, or substitute for attentive adult supervision.

## Supervision and Physical Safety

A responsible adult should supervise use. Secure the device and surrounding area. Keep charging cables, detachable accessories, damaged screens, batteries, and small parts away from children. Do not allow unsafe chewing, throwing, striking, climbing, or prolonged unsupported holding of the device.

Screen Pinning and Guided Access can reduce accidental navigation but are operating-system conveniences, not guaranteed childproof security. Adults remain responsible for device access, purchases, communications, settings, and external content.

## Visual, Audio, Motion, and Haptic Effects

The App contains animated colors, particles, moving shapes, sound, local speech, and optional vibration. It is designed to avoid aggressive strobing, but no visual application can guarantee comfort or safety for every person. Stop immediately if a user experiences discomfort, eyestrain, headache, dizziness, nausea, agitation, unusual behavior, or seizure-like symptoms. Seek appropriate medical assistance when warranted.

Set volume and screen brightness to comfortable levels. Avoid placing speakers close to a child’s ears. Use age-appropriate session lengths and allow breaks.

## Operational Limitations

Rendering, sound, speech, haptics, motion input, screen locking, and saved settings may vary or fail because of device hardware, operating-system behavior, installed voices, accessibility configuration, low-memory conditions, battery restrictions, interruptions, updates, or software defects.

Do not rely on the App in any safety-critical setting or as the sole means of calming, occupying, monitoring, educating, treating, or supervising a child.
