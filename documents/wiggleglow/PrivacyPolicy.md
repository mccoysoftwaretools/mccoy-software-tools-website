# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: August 15, 2026**  
**Document version: 1.1.0**

This Privacy Policy explains how WiggleGlow (“the App”), provided by McCoy Software Tools LLC (“we,” “us,” or “our”), handles information. The App is designed for parents and caregivers to configure an offline sensory-play experience for young children.

## Summary

The current version of WiggleGlow does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, behavioral tracking, social features, or developer-operated network services. We do not knowingly collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information through the App.

## Information Stored on the Device

The App stores limited settings locally on the device, including the selected activity, visual and sound preferences, accessibility or child-safe-mode preferences, and the version of the legal documents accepted by the parent or caregiver. These settings are not transmitted to us.

The operating system or app store may independently process purchase, installation, crash, diagnostic, backup, device, or usage information under its own terms and privacy policy. We do not control information processed independently by Apple, Google, a device manufacturer, mobile carrier, or another system service.

## Permissions and Device Features

The App may use vibration or haptic feedback, local text-to-speech, device motion when enabled by the parent, screen pinning on Android, and Guided Access status on iOS. The App does not request access to the camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, or photo library.

Local text-to-speech is supplied by the device operating system. Availability and voice processing behavior may depend on the installed operating-system voice. WiggleGlow does not send spoken labels to a developer-operated server.

## Children’s Privacy

WiggleGlow is designed to minimize children’s data exposure. It does not ask a child to provide a name, birthday, email address, photo, voice recording, location, account identifier, or other personal information. It contains no child-facing external links, purchases, advertising, messaging, or user-generated content.

The parent-facing area contains legal and app-store links. Parents and caregivers are responsible for supervising access to those areas and for configuring operating-system parental controls appropriate for their family.

Because the App does not knowingly collect personal information from children, there is no child profile or developer-held child data to review or delete. If you believe information was provided to us outside the App, contact us so we can evaluate and address the request.

## Data Retention and Deletion

Local preferences remain on the device until they are changed, cleared through operating-system settings, or removed by uninstalling the App. Because the current App does not transmit these preferences to us, we cannot remotely access or delete them.

## Security

No system can be guaranteed completely secure. WiggleGlow reduces risk by avoiding accounts, remote storage, analytics, advertising SDKs, and developer-operated data transmission. Device security, backups, and access controls remain the responsibility of the device owner.

## Changes

We may update this policy when the App’s functionality, legal requirements, or data practices change. Material changes will increment the bundled document version and may require renewed in-app acceptance.
