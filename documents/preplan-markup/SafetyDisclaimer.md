# Safety Disclaimer

**Effective date:** August 15, 2026
**Version:** 2

Preplan Markup is a drafting and organizational aid. It is not an official inspection, code-compliance, engineering, dispatch, incident-command, emergency-response, or life-safety system.

Conditions at a property can change at any time. Always verify access points, utilities, hazards, fire-protection systems, building features, labels, and all other preplan information against current site conditions and your organization’s approved procedures before operational use.

Symbols and text in the app communicate only the meaning assigned by the person or organization that created the preplan. The app does not validate whether a symbol is correct, current, complete, accurately positioned, or consistent with an agency standard.

Do not delay emergency action to access or troubleshoot the app. Do not rely on the app as the sole source of information during an emergency. Current observations, dispatch information, incident command, approved records, and agency procedures take priority.

This app is designed to assist in the collection of data for export and therefore usage elsewhere and is not designed itself to ever be used or relied on in an emergency.

The user and the user’s organization are responsible for reviewing, approving, distributing, securing, updating, and retiring every preplan created with the app.
