# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 15, 2026
**Version:** 2

## Overview

Preplan Markup (“the App”) is provided by McCoy Software Tools LLC and is designed to work locally on your device. The App does not currently provide user accounts, advertising, analytics, developer-operated cloud storage, or background collection of personal information.

## Information stored by the app

The app stores preplan projects, imported symbols, app settings, and legal-acceptance status in its private local application storage. Project data can include images and text that you choose to add.

## Photos and camera access

The app accesses a photo or camera image only when you choose or capture one for a preplan. The app creates a private working copy so the original image remains unchanged.

## Exporting and sharing

An image leaves the app’s private storage only when you direct the app to save it to the device gallery or invoke the operating system’s share sheet. A destination app, service, employer, or device-management system may apply separate privacy and retention practices.

## Network activity

The app may open external legal-document links at your request using the device browser. The current app does not otherwise transmit project contents to the developer.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains until you delete a project, remove imported content, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the app. Deleting the app, losing the device, or experiencing storage failure may permanently remove local projects. Gallery exports and externally shared copies must be deleted from their respective destinations separately.

## Children’s privacy

The app is intended as a professional productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13. The developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Security

The app relies on operating-system application storage protections. No storage method is guaranteed to prevent every loss or unauthorized access. Use device security controls and follow your organization’s policies for sensitive site information.

## Policy changes

This policy will be updated before new data practices are introduced. A material change may require renewed acceptance through the app’s Legal Disclosures screen.
